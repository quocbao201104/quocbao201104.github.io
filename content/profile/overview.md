# Professional Profile — Quoc Bao

Quoc Bao is a Vietnam-based **Backend & AI Systems Engineer** focused on operationally reliable backend systems, bounded AI workflows, durable state, recovery, observability, security boundaries, and evidence-aware agent behavior.

## Positioning
The strongest recurring pattern in Bao's work is not a specific framework. It is designing systems that must behave predictably outside the happy path: retries, restarts, duplicate work, stale state, partial failure, authorization boundaries, external effects, evidence, uncertainty, and recovery.

Good short answer: Bao builds backend and AI systems where state transitions are explicit, automation is bounded, and failures or uncertain evidence are handled deliberately instead of hidden behind fluent output.
